Limits

  • Chosen after the fact, as above. The holdout from 25 Jan 2026 is the only stretch the detector never saw, and it is short.
  • Survivorship: the data holds shares that still have records; shares that were delisted may be missing, which flatters any buy strategy.
  • Fills: a buy at the next open assumes enough sellers at that price. Circuit limits, the 2020 floor-price period, queues and taxes are not modelled. The 0.80% and 1.30% rows show how fast a thin edge disappears.
  • Overlap: the portfolio drawdown only moves when a trade is sold, so the true worst drop was deeper.
